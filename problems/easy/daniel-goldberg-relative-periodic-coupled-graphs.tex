\end{center}

\section{Definitions}

Let
\[
  \mathbb T=\mathbb R/(2\pi\mathbb Z).
\]
Let smooth periodic functions $u,v$ solve
\begin{equation}
  u_t
  =
  \partial_x\left(\frac{u_x}{\sqrt{1+u_x^2}}\right)
  -(u-v),
  \qquad
  v_t
  =
  \partial_x\left(\frac{v_x}{\sqrt{1+v_x^2}}\right)
  +(u-v).
\end{equation}
Suppose that, for some
\begin{equation}
  T>0,
  \qquad
  a\in\mathbb T,
  \qquad
  b\in\mathbb R,
\end{equation}
one has
\begin{equation}
  u(x,T)=u(x+a,0)+b,
  \qquad
  v(x,T)=v(x+a,0)+b.
\end{equation}

\section{Best Known Result}

Horizontal translations and common vertical translations are symmetries of
the functional
\begin{equation}
  \mathcal L(u,v)
  =
  \int_{\mathbb T}
  \left(
    \sqrt{1+u_x^2}
    +\sqrt{1+v_x^2}
    +\frac{1}{2}(u-v)^2
  \right)dx.
\end{equation}
The source indicates that an exact Lyapunov identity, together with rigidity
of the critical points, should determine the classification. It does not
provide the resulting classification.

\section{Problem Formalization}


Classify all such smooth solutions and all possible triples $(a,b,T)$.
