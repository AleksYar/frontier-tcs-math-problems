\section{Preliminaries}

A proof system $P$ polynomially simulates a proof system $Q$ if there is a
polynomial $p$ such that every $Q$-refutation of size $s$ has a
$P$-refutation of the same formula of size at most $p(s)$.

Let $\Qinf=\mathbb{Q}\cup\{\infty\}$ with
$a\oplus b=\min\{a,b\}$ and $a\odot b=a+b$.
For every Boolean variable $x$, introduce tropical variables $x,\bar x$ and
the equations
\[
 x\odot\bar x=1,
 \qquad
 x\oplus\bar x=0.
\]
A clause $\ell_1\vee\cdots\vee\ell_k$ is encoded by
$1\leq\ell_1\odot\cdots\odot\ell_k$.

An $\MPNSR$ refutation is an algebraic combination
\[
 \bigoplus_j t_j\odot f_j
 \;\leq\;
 \bigoplus_j t_j\odot g_j,
\]
where $f_j\leq g_j$ ranges over the encoded clauses, both directions of the
Boolean equations, the axiom $0\leq0$, and inequalities $g\leq\infty$ for
right-hand sides $g$ of the preceding input inequalities.  It is a refutation
if every monomial on the left has a strictly larger
coefficient than the same monomial on the right and the right-hand side has a
finite constant term.

A $\ResLP$ line is a finite disjunction of integer affine inequalities.  It
starts from the usual linear encodings of clauses and the Boolean axioms
\[
 x\geq0,
 \qquad
 1-x\geq0,
 \qquad
 (x-1\geq0)\vee(-x\geq0),
\]
and uses
\[
\frac{(f_1\geq0)\vee\Gamma\qquad(f_2\geq0)\vee\Gamma}
     {(\alpha_1f_1+\alpha_2f_2\geq0)\vee\Gamma},
\qquad
\frac{\Gamma}{\Gamma\vee(f\geq0)},
\]
where $\alpha_1,\alpha_2$ are positive integers.  A refutation derives
$-1\geq0$.
A $\ResLP$ refutation is \emph{treelike} if every derived line is used as a
premise at most once.
Proof size is the total binary encoding length.

\section{Problem formulation}

Does treelike $\ResLP$ polynomially simulate $\MPNSR$?  Namely, is there a
polynomial $p$ such that every $\MPNSR$ refutation of size $s$ can be
converted into a treelike $\ResLP$ refutation of size at most
\[
 p(s)?
\]
