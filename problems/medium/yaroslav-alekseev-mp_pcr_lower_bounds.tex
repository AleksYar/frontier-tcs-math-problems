\section{Preliminaries}

Let
\[
\Qinf:=\mathbb{Q}\cup\{\infty\},
\qquad
 a\oplus b:=\min\{a,b\},
\qquad
 a\odot b:=a+b,
\]
with $a\oplus\infty=a$ and $a\odot\infty=\infty$.
A \emph{tropical term} is an expression
\[
 c\odot x_1^{\odot d_1}\odot\cdots\odot x_n^{\odot d_n},
\]
where $c\in\Qinf$ and $d_1,\ldots,d_n\in\mathbb{N}_0$.
A \emph{tropical polynomial} is a finite tropical sum of tropical terms.
A \emph{min-plus inequality} is an inequality $f\leq g$ between tropical
polynomials.

For every Boolean variable $x$, introduce two tropical variables $x$ and
$\bar x$, together with the equations
\[
 x\odot\bar x=1,
 \qquad
 x\oplus\bar x=0.
\]
Each equation is treated as the two opposite inequalities.  A Boolean clause
$\ell_1\vee\cdots\vee\ell_k$ is encoded by
\[
 1\leq \ell_1\odot\cdots\odot\ell_k,
\]
where the tropical variable corresponding to the literal $\neg x$ is $\bar x$.

An $\MPPCR$ derivation is a sequence of min-plus inequalities.  Every line is
an encoded input clause, a Boolean axiom, an axiom $0\leq0$ or $p\leq\infty$,
or follows from earlier lines by one of the rules
\[
\frac{p\leq q\qquad p'\leq q'}{p\oplus p'\leq q\oplus q'},
\qquad
\frac{p\leq q}{p\odot t\leq q\odot t},
\qquad
\frac{p\leq h\qquad h\leq r}{p\leq r},
\]
where $t$ is a tropical term.

For tropical polynomials $p,q$, write $p\succ q$ if, for every monomial
occurring in $p$, the coefficient of that monomial in $p$ is strictly larger
than its coefficient in $q$.  An $\MPPCR$ refutation ends with an inequality
$p\leq q$ such that $p\succ q$ and $q$ has a finite constant term.
The size of a derivation is the total binary encoding length of all its lines.

A family $(F_n)_{n\geq1}$ of CNF formulas is \emph{explicit} if $F_n$ can be
constructed in time polynomial in $n$.

\section{Problem formulation}

Construct an explicit family $(F_n)_{n\geq1}$ of unsatisfiable CNF formulas
of size $n^{O(1)}$ such that every $\MPPCR$ refutation of $F_n$ has
superpolynomial size:
\[
 \operatorname{size}_{\MPPCR}(F_n)=n^{\omega(1)}.
\]
