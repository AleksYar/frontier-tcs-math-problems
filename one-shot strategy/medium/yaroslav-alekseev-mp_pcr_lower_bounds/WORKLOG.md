This extraction is fully sound. It also pinpoints the obstruction: lower bounds for these DAGs are open already for intersections of two triangles (equivalent to EQ-2-dags and strong enough to imply major daglike Res(Lin) lower bounds). The conversion therefore supplies a rigorous intermediate characterization, not the desired lower bound.
