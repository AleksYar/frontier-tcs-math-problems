- 2026-08-28T13:30:53Z (corrected 13:32Z): Verified constructive bridge lemma (used by the PDF but not proved there): a directed elimination forest of height `k` can be converted in polynomial time to an equivalent regular-expression circuit of star height at most `k` (up to an inessential boundary convention). Thus a constructive forest approximation suffices for the conjecture, and a conjectured circuit at least gives a value approximation.
