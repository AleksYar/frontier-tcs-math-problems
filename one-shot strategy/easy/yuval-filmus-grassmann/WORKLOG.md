- **11:46+02:00 — Major correction: a rank-four and all-ranks primitive family.** The claim that quadratic mechanisms stop at rank four was too broad: it is true for *nondegenerate elliptic* forms but false for rank-two hyperbolic forms. Let \(a,b\in V^*\) be independent and let \(z\in\mathbb F_2^2\setminus\{0\}\). For the affine codimension-two coset
  \[
  C_z=\{x\in V:(a(x),b(x))=z\},

  the restriction of \((a,b)\) to a \(k\)-space has rank \(0,1\), or \(2\). If \(z\) is not in its image the intersection is empty; if the image is the line \(\langle z\rangle\) the intersection has \(2^{k-1}\) points; if the image is all of \(\mathbb F_2^2\) it has \(2^{k-2}\) points. This family survives with \(\min(k,n-k)\) arbitrarily large, so **no choice of the allowed constant \(t\) can make “sum of two Boolean functions” the full classification**.

- **11:48+02:00 — Rigorous non-splitting certificate for the affine-coset family (audited and corrected).** Canonical point coefficients are unique because the point-versus-\(k\)-space incidence matrix has positive-definite Gram matrix \((r-\lambda)I+\lambda J\). A sum of two supplied Boolean functions has, away from at most two exceptional point deltas, coefficient form \(A+B(-1)^{r(x)}+C(-1)^{s(x)}\). The coefficient of \(F_z\) is \(2^{2-k}\mathbf1_{C_z}(x)\), whose Fourier expansion uses the four distinct characters \(1,\chi_a,\chi_b,\chi_{a+b}\). Their difference has Fourier support of size at most seven, while equality away from the two deltas and the zero vector would give physical support at most three. The finite-group uncertainty inequality \(|\operatorname{supp}D|\,|\operatorname{supp}\widehat D|\ge2^n\) rules this out for \(n\ge5\); if \(D=0\), uniqueness of the Fourier expansion rules it out because all four coset-indicator coefficients are nonzero. (The initially noted Reed–Muller justification was inapplicable when \(r,s\) have arbitrary Hamming weight and has been discarded.) Thus \(F_z\) is not a Boolean pair sum under the high-rank hypothesis.

- **11:54+02:00 — Quotient-invariant classification proved in arbitrary quotient dimension.** Suppose the point coefficient \(c_x\) depends only on \(A(x)\in E=\mathbb F_2^d\), for a surjection \(A:V\to E\), and every image subspace can occur (e.g. \(k\ge d\) and the ambient kernel is large enough). If the fiber weights are \(w_z\), define \(\phi(z)=2^k w_z-w_0\). For a \(k\)-space with image \(R\le E\), direct fiber counting gives
  \[
  f(L)=|R|^{-1}\sum_{z\in R}\phi(z).
  \]
  Hence every linear-subspace average of \(\phi\) lies in \(\{0,1,2\}\). This auxiliary problem has a complete elementary classification:
  * If \(\phi(0)=0\), line and plane averages show that \(\phi/2\pmod2\) is a linear functional. In the zero-parity case the nonzero support is sum-free; the subspace-average condition and Parseval force a nonempty primitive support to be an affine codimension-two coset (or a maximum sum-free affine hyperplane, which is a doubled Boolean function). In the nonzero-parity case the remaining even part is either empty or an affine hyperplane inside the kernel, giving a sum of two Boolean hyperplane-complement functions.
  * The case \(\phi(0)=2\) follows by \(\phi\mapsto2-\phi\).
  * If \(\phi(0)=1\), then \((\phi-1)/2\in\{-1,0,1\}\). Plane parities force its support to be an affine hyperplane. On every affine 2-plane of that support, the sign pattern has even parity, hence is an affine character. The resulting averages are \(1\), \(1\pm(1-y_r)\), or \(1+y_r-y_s\), all Boolean pair sums.
  Consequently, in every quotient-invariant family the only non-pair primitives are \(F_z\) and \(2-F_z\). This analytically explains and extends both exact quotient enumerations.

- **12:11–12:14+02:00 — Exact local cubic equations derived and verified.** Put \(P(X)=X(X-1)(X-2)\). Expand \(P(f)\) by the dimension spanned by an ordered tuple of points. For a line \(\ell\) and a 3-space \(T\), write
  \[
  B_\ell=\Big(\sum_{p\in\ell}c_p\Big)^3-\sum_{p\in\ell}c_p^3,\qquad
  D_\ell=\Big(\sum_{p\in\ell}c_p\Big)^2-\sum_{p\in\ell}c_p^2,
  \]
  and
  \[
  C_T=\Big(\sum_{p\in T}c_p\Big)^3-\sum_{\ell\le T}B_\ell-\sum_{p\in T}c_p^3.
  \]
  If \(3\le k\le n-3\), real full row rank of the 3-space-to-\(k\)-space incidence matrix shows that \(f\) is ternary **iff**, for every \(T\),
  \[
  C_T+\frac1{2^{k-2}-1}\sum_{\ell\le T}(B_\ell-3D_\ell)
   +\frac1{{k-1\brack2}_2}\sum_{p\in T}(c_p^3-3c_p^2+2c_p)=0. \tag{LC}
  \]
  Sufficiency follows by reversing the lift and obtaining \(P(f)=0\); necessity follows from incidence injectivity. `tmp/audit_cubic_local.py` checked (LC) with exact fractions on all 97,155 three-spaces of \(\mathbb F_2^8\) for the affine-coset core (zero failures).
